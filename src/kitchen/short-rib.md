Five attempts at this went wrong in the same way: the fat never rendered
properly, so the sauce came out greasy instead of glossy.

The fix was searing the meat straight from the fridge instead of letting it come
to room temperature first. Cold meat can sit in the pan longer before the
interior starts cooking, which means the fat cap actually has time to render
instead of just browning.

## Method

Salt the ribs the night before, uncovered in the fridge. Sear cold, hard, on all
sides. Build the braise on the fond, return the meat, and hold it at a bare
simmer for three hours — if it's bubbling, it's too hot.
