---
title: The quiet speeches land harder
date: 2026-07-30
blurb: Restraint reads as more urgent than volume.
---

I've been rereading speeches this year, and the pattern I keep noticing is that
the ones that stay with me are almost never the rousing ones.

The rousing speech tells you how to feel. The quiet one trusts you to get there
yourself, and that trust is what makes it land — you arrive at the conclusion
under your own power, so it's yours.

This bleeds into how I write postmortems at work. The instinct is to state the
severity loudly. But the incident report that actually changes behavior is the
one that lays out what happened plainly and lets the reader draw the obvious
conclusion.
